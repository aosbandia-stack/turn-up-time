Required outputs:
- entities, ownership, and invariants;
- CRUD and lifecycle expectations;
- API/service contracts;
- sync/async boundaries;
- idempotency, retry, duplicate, timeout, and partial-failure behavior;
- integrations and versioning;
- observability and user-derived reliability targets;
- migration, deployment, rollback, and recovery;
- tests that prove every invariant;
- evidence labels, sources, and applicability.
